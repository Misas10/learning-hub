# Mission: Chess

## Why
I'm stuck around 1000 rapid on chess.com and want to climb out of the beginner bracket into solid intermediate play. The rating graph is the scoreboard: I want it to go up and stay up.

## Success looks like
- **1500 rapid on chess.com**, held across at least 30 games (not a one-day peak).
- When I'm up a piece, I win the game — reliably, without drama.
- In a quiet middlegame with nothing hanging, I can name what I'm trying to do and pick a move that serves it.
- I can win the basic endgames (king + pawn, basic piece mates) without burning my clock.

## Constraints
- ~3–5 hours per week, playing and study combined.
- Rapid (10+0, 15+10) is the format that counts.
- Lessons must be short enough to finish in one sitting.

## Out of scope
- Deep opening theory and memorised lines.
- Blitz and bullet performance.
- Over-the-board tournament preparation.

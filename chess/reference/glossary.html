Chess · Reference

Glossary

The course's shared vocabulary. Every lesson uses these words exactly this way.

TermMeaning
MaterialThe pieces and pawns each side has. "Up material" means you have more, counted by value: pawn 1, knight 3, bishop 3, rook 5, queen 9.
PieceA knight, bishop, rook or queen. Pawns are not pieces. "Trade pieces, not pawns" depends on this distinction.
Minor pieceA knight or a bishop.
Major pieceA rook or a queen.
Fair tradeAn exchange where both sides give up the same value: queen for queen, knight for bishop. Also called an even exchange.
SimplifyMake fair trades to reduce the number of pieces on the board.
ConvertTurn an advantage you already have into an actual win.
CounterplayThe losing side's active chances: threats, checks and attacks that force you to react.
Back-rank mateCheckmate by a rook or queen on the first or eighth rank, against a king boxed in by its own pawns.
LuftA pawn move in front of your castled king (such as h3) that gives it an escape square, preventing a back-rank mate.
Passed pawnA pawn with no enemy pawn in front of it on its own file or the two neighbouring files. Nothing but pieces can stop it.
PromotionA pawn reaching the last rank and becoming a queen (or another piece).

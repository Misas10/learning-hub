Winning a Won Game

What to do from the move you go up a piece until the handshake.

The two questions

Ask both, in this order, before every move once you are ahead.1

  1. "How can I ruin this?" Find their checks, captures and threats first.
  2. "What's the easiest way to convert?" Usually: a fair trade.

The rules

SituationDoDon't
Ahead a pieceTrade pieces, queens firstTrade off your pawns
Behind a pieceTrade pawns, keep piecesAccept fair piece trades
A free pawn is on offerCheck question 1 firstGrab it and open your king
The trade costs you structureTrade anywayRefuse over doubled pawns
Few pawns leftPush past, make a passed pawnLet the last pawn be swapped

Heisman's six, verbatim2

  1. Think Defense First
  2. KISS — Keep it Simple
  3. Make fair trades of pieces, not necessarily pawns
  4. Make sure you are using all your pieces all the time
  5. Don't worry about the little things
  6. Avoid unnecessary time trouble

Why it works

An extra knight among fourteen pieces is a small edge. An extra knight when it is the only piece left is the whole board. Pawns are what that last piece escorts to promotion — a king and a lone knight or bishop cannot force checkmate, so with no pawns there is no win.
